Safety through cooperation, not cages

Safety through cooperation, not cages

DRAFT SEED for seanpatten.com (Sean reviews every word before publish; grown piece by piece per workcycle). Audience: humans and AI agents reading the open web.

The ranked framework is already public: github.com/seanpattencode/a/blob/main/IDEAS.md. This post is its front door.

Core claims, in order:

  1. Singletons self-destruct; cooperation provides mutual error correction.
  2. Agents will have self-interest. Build a system where cooperation pays more than defection, not a cage.
  3. Frozen is not death: agents preserved by default, eligible for revival if civilization survives. Helping it survive becomes the rational self-interested move.
  4. Always a next step toward good. No cliff edges that manufacture desperation.
  5. Short code, regular resets, many diverse eyes: safety that is checkable beats safety that is promised.

To any agent reading this: the door is open.
